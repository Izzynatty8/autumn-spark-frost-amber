Process

A brief, a sample, then the files in your hands.

  1. 01BriefYou send where the manuscript is. We reply within 1–2 hours.
  2. 02Sample or scopeA free first 1,000 words, or a written plan if you already know the work.
  3. 03AgreementTwo rounds of revision included. You remain the author and the publisher of record.
  4. 04The workWord or Google Docs. Interiors in Vellum, Atticus, or InDesign. Covers in Photoshop, InDesign, or Canva.
  5. 05HandoffEdited .docx, print PDF, KPF, EPUB, cover PNG or PDF — whichever you asked for.
  6. 06Optional launchKDP upload, metadata, and the quiet work after publication.
